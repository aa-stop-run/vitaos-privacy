VitaOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

VitaOS was designed from the ground up with absolute privacy and ownership of your data as its founding principles.

1.100% local-first architecture (offline)

  • VitaOS has no remote servers, no cloud database, and no telemetry or tracking of any kind.
  • All your data — including account balances, transactions, workout routines, medical appointments, medications, vehicles and identity documents — is stored only on your device, in a local SQLite database.
  • Every feature of the app works offline. The only network access is Google Play Billing, used solely to process VitaOS Pro purchases — and none of your data goes through it.
  • Google's automatic cloud backup is turned off (android:allowBackup="false"), so your data is never synced to Google Drive without your knowledge. As a result, your data does not move to a new phone by itself — use Settings → Change phone to take your backup file with you.
  • The database lives in the app's private storage, out of reach of other apps. It is not encrypted: the protection comes from the data never leaving your phone, not from encryption.

2.No third-party tracking, no advertising

  • VitaOS contains no ads (no AdMob or any other ad network).
  • VitaOS contains no behavioural tracking tools and no invasive analytics.

3.Billing and payments

  • Optional VitaOS Pro subscription or licence purchases are processed solely by the official Google Play Billing service.
  • Your payment details (credit cards, PayPal, MB Way) are handled directly and securely by Google Play. VitaOS never accesses or stores any card numbers or banking credentials.

4.Device permissions

  • No dangerous storage permissions: VitaOS does not ask for access to your device's shared storage. CSV and JSON exports go to Android's native share sheet, where you choose where to save the file.
  • PIN protection: the optional lock uses a PIN protected with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and a random salt generated per installation, with an attempt limit, escalating lockouts and automatic locking when the app goes to the background. The PIN never leaves the device — not even in backup files.

5.Local reminders

  • Reminders are created on your phone, from the data stored there. Nothing is sent anywhere to produce them, and no notification server is involved.
  • They are off by default. They only exist once you turn them on in Settings → Reminders, and turning them off immediately cancels everything that was scheduled.
  • Medication reminders are marked as private: on a secured lock screen (PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face), Android hides their content and only shows that there is a notification. This keeps the name of a medicine out of sight of anyone nearby. On a phone without a screen lock, the system shows the content, and there is nothing the app can do about that.
  • Reminders for deadlines, appointments and documents never include in their text the number of an identity document, the doctor's name, the medical specialty or any notes you have written.
  • By deliberate choice, reminders use inexact alarms: the time can shift by a few minutes, in exchange for saving battery and not asking for restricted permissions. They should not be your only way of remembering to take a dose.

6.Your data belongs to you

  • You can export a complete copy of your data in JSON from Settings at any time. With VitaOS Pro, you can also export it as CSV.
  • You can restore a previously exported JSON backup.
  • You can permanently delete all information from the device at any time with Settings → Clear All Data.
